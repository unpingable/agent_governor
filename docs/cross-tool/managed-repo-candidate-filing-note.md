# Cross-tool custody note: managed-repo candidate filings are AG-authorized, home-repo-local

## Status

**PROVISIONAL custody/jurisdiction note.** Filed 2026-06-18 (operator + ChatGPT,
during the kernel-arch porting pass). Records *where* cross-repo doctrine candidates
for AG-governed tools may be filed and under whose authority. Routine register —
this note declares a custody convention; it does not itself admit any architecture
into any repo.

## The fog this dissolves

When AG recognizes that one of its kernel mechanisms belongs (in local grammar) in
a sibling office — continuity, standing, NQ, wicket, LA — the recurring instinct is
to stage the note inside agent_gov first, or to ask each repo for permission "like
tiny feudal barons." That instinct is correct in a generic multi-repo setting and
**wrong here**, because it misses one local fact:

> **AG is already the cross-repo custody root for the governed constellation.**

AG has constellation-governor authority over the managed tools. That authority does
**not** collapse their office boundaries — it is precisely the authority to author
cross-repo doctrine movement *while keeping each office sovereign over its own
doctrine.*

## The rule

> **Managed-repo doctrine candidates may be filed in their home repositories under
> AG custody, provided they are explicitly non-binding and do not authorize
> implementation unless separately admitted.**

Decomposed:

- **AG is the authorizing / custody office**, not the storage location. Do not stage
  a candidate inside agent_gov merely because it touches another repo.
- **The candidate belongs in the office repo whose seam it describes.** continuity
  owns continuity doctrine; standing owns standing doctrine; NQ owns
  witness/preflight doctrine; wicket owns receiver/admission doctrine. AG authorizes
  the *movement*; the office owns the *doctrine*.
- **Each filed note is doc-only / candidate / non-binding:** no implementation
  authorized, no architecture admitted, no transition-kernel port, no authority
  collapse between offices.
- If the loop requires cross-repo custody evidence, AG adds a backlink / receipt;
  the note still lives in the home repo.

## Authority over the constellation, not merger of offices

```text
AG:         authorizes / governs cross-repo doctrine movement (custody root)
continuity: owns continuity doctrine
standing:   owns standing doctrine
NQ:         owns witness / preflight doctrine
wicket:     owns receiver / admission doctrine
LA:         owns capacity / exactly-once doctrine
```

This is the same boundary as the microkernel note
(`receipt-sovereignty-microkernel-note.md`): *co-locate the repos; never merge the
authorities.* Authoring a candidate into a sibling repo is co-location of doctrine
under one custody root — not a merge of the offices' authority.

## Doctrine lines

- AG is the cross-repo custody root; it authorizes filings, it is not their warehouse.
- Authority over the constellation is not merger of offices.
- A candidate belongs in the office whose seam it describes.
- AG-authorized, home-repo-local, doc-only, non-binding.
