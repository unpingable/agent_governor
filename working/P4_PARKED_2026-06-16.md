# P4 — PARKED (architecturally premature) — 2026-06-16

Operator ruling, cold-from-receipts, on the consumer-reachability audit
(`working/P4-tunable-consumer-reachability-audit-2026-06-16.md`). **P4 is parked.**

## Why parked (not "blocked on a TODO")

The audit did not uncover a missing implementation detail. It **falsified the
campaign's current premise**:

> AG can govern activation, but activation currently governs nothing operational.

Wiring a consumer now would build a *referent* so the already-built governance
machinery has something to govern. That may eventually be legitimate — but doing it
**to rescue P4 would reverse the dependency**: the trial would be *choosing* the
actuator instead of *evaluating* an independently-needed one. A trial that selects
its own subject is not a trial.

> Better a conspicuously unused constitutional mechanism than a Potemkin actuator
> built so the receipts have somewhere to go.

## Parked state of record

- **P4 trial-evidence path:** BLOCKED, architecturally premature.
- **Promotion chamber:** intentionally empty (`.governor/control_baselines/` and
  `.governor/promotion_evidence/` absent — by design, the successful 3c shape).
- **Activation subsystem:** valid custody machinery (four-office
  admissibility · standing · exactly-once spend · durable custody, tested), **no
  operational consumers.** It is an actuator registry awaiting demand.
- **P4 cannot resume** until a real control surface *independently* adopts
  `ActiveTunableStore`.
- **A first-consumer project is NOT part of P4** and receives **no authority** from
  these receipts. If one happens, it is its own campaign with its own admission.
- **Explicitly rejected rescue candidates:** `backoff_base` (live consumer but
  hardcoded value, disconnected from activation, wrong authority class — MCP
  rate-limiting, not self-governance) and `decomposition_size/max_slices` (no
  slice-count executor; making it operational = a decomposition-authority project).
  Neither may be rehabilitated to give P4 a specimen.

## Reopening condition (strict — all clauses)

> A production control surface has an **independently justified** need for
> runtime-governed configuration, AND its **owning campaign** chooses
> `ActiveTunableStore` because the **custody model fits** — not because P4 needs a
> specimen.

At that point — and only then — P4 returns and trials *that* actuator (the one the
owning campaign adopted on its own merits). Until then the registry is infrastructure
awaiting demand, and that is the correct resting state.

## What stays true while parked
- The activation/promotion machinery does not regress: it is real, tested, and on
  `origin/main`. Parking is an orientation signal, not a deletion.
- The three boundary commits behind this ruling preserve the negative results so a
  later first-consumer campaign cannot retroactively launder them into "P4 evidence":
  `02bec1b` (inert tunable) · `107e51d` (no slice executor) · `c2499e9` (audit:
  actuator-registry-without-actuators).

Resume discipline: do NOT reopen P4 on momentum or to "finish the campaign." The
reopening condition is a demand signal from elsewhere, not a P4 decision.
