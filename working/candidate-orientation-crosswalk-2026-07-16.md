## The claim

There is a judgment family below admissibility: **orientation** — artifacts
that may change what a system notices, suggests, and prioritizes
(salience / suggestion / priority) while remaining structurally unable to
write certification, probe authority, or action authority. The ladder gains
a lower rung:

    notice/suggest ≠ investigate ≠ believe(certify) ≠ approve ≠ act

Two theorems carry content the estate did not previously have in formal
shape:

1. **`protected_frame_preservation_does_not_imply_fair_inquiry`** — a
   hostile signal can preserve every certification and authority bit while
   deleting counter-hypotheses and falsifying probes from attention.
   Authority noninterference is necessary and *insufficient*; "no
   laundering" theorems do not defend inquiry integrity. Epistemic steering
   is a different attack than authority laundering.
2. **`change_localizes_to_privileged`** (Attribution) — in a mixed trace,
   any protected-frame change decomposes at a privileged step that itself
   changes it at its own input state. Orientation can propagate a change,
   never originate one. This is the formal shape of "every effect names its
   authorizing receipt."
